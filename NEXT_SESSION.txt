5. IMPLEMENTED — STEP-BY-STEP FABRICATION GUIDE
The committed designer now has a compact Fabrication guide button below the viewer.
It starts from the flat stencil. < / > advance and reverse ONE actual manufacturing operation:
layout -> half-fold about E-J -> longitudinal seal -> inlet -> open pouch into box -> inward side folds and flatten -> front seal -> rear seal.
Seal stages highlight paired coincident ideal guide lines and show Seal here.
Normal Play/Fold remains a separate actuation preview. Exiting restores the exact previous viewer, camera and actuation state.
Guide mode temporarily makes design editing inert; orbit/pan/zoom still work.

Architecture:
- kernel/fabrication-guide.mjs owns stage ordering/instructions, rigid panel maps, film allowance guides, seal pairs, inlet and fixed viewing envelope.
- API methods fabrication.describe, fabrication.pose, fabrication.validate carry JSON through the existing Worker.
- ui/fabrication-player.mjs owns only navigation/timing and cancellation. Every intermediate pose comes from the API, with no vertex interpolation in the UI.
- ui/fabrication-render.mjs draws returned semantic cues, with no fold equations or hardcoded stage sequence.
- ui/block-app.mjs only connects those modules to the editor and restores state on exit.
- docs/fabrication-guide.md records the geometric construction and limitations.

Geometric construction (ordinary rectangular, two-wall, equal-rise box only):
Half folding leaves the top translated by -2l in x relative to bottom; it is NOT the old collapsed actuation pose.
After longitudinal sealing, translate the top by d=(-2l*cos(theta),0,2l*sin(theta)), theta 0..pi/2, with side midhinges at d/2 relative to corresponding bottom corners.
This opens a closed parallelogram with straight two-panel walls into the upright box without changing any panel metric or opening the first seam.
Then use the inward branch from opening=1 down to 0. At the flattened endpoint, paired top/bottom end sealing lines actually coincide at y=-(l+c) and y=L+l+c.
The two end seals are performed at this flattened configuration, not with separated lines on an open box.
Other geometries explicitly return unsupported diagnostics. Do not invent their manufacturing paths.
Old assembly() and design.pose assembly are retained as legacy synchronous interpolation, not used by this guide.

Evidence:
- 72 Node tests passed, plus every standalone legacy regression group.
- Four rectangular dimension/gap/thickness sets checked at 151 samples per stage for vertex distances, areas, hinge endpoints, closed seam, sealing endpoints and transition continuity; no interior zero-thickness crossings found.
- Default 51-sample errors were about 1.8e-14 mm (distance), 1.4e-12 mm^2 (area), 1.2e-14 mm (seam), 1.2e-14 mm (transition).
- Real browser: all 8 stages advanced, last Next disabled, all 7 reversals returned byte-identical starting SVG, exit returned byte-identical original preview.
- Test log: ../outputs/Fabrication_Guide_Tests.log (outside repository).

Limits:
All paths use ideal zero-thickness mid-surfaces. Flat stacking is deliberate; positive-thickness offsets/compliant hinges are not solved.
Translucent top/bottom film allowance strips are schematic. Complete membrane/gusset folding, inlet hardware, airtight seal widths and inflation after end sealing are not simulated.
Matched ideal guide endpoints are verified; do not call this a finite-thickness manufacturing certificate.
